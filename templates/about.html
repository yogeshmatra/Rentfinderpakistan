Our Mission & Story

Transforming Rentals in Pakistan

We are on a mission to build Pakistan's most transparent, high-tech, and scam-free rental property marketplace.

Who We Are

Rent Finder Pakistan was founded to solve a massive daily problem faced by millions of Pakistani families, students, and professionals: the frustrating and opaque process of finding a house, flat, or commercial space for rent.

Traditional property portals treat rental listings as an afterthought behind high-value plot sales. We are 100% dedicated to rentals. Every feature on our platform—from Sui gas pressure indicators and water boring notes to loadshedding backup details and advance payment terms—is tailored specifically for Pakistani renters and landlords.

The Three Pillars of Rent Finder Pakistan

Human Moderation

No auto-publishing without verification. Our team manually approves listings to ensure verified phone numbers and accurate rents.

Gemini AI Assistant

Our conversational AI chatbot understands English, Urdu and Roman Urdu to match renters with the best properties in seconds.

Direct Connections

Instant WhatsApp chat and direct phone calling with landlords. Zero hidden intermediary commission fees.
