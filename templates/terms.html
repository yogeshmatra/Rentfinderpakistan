Terms of Service & Pakistani Tenancy Guidelines

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Marketplace Nature

Rent Finder Pakistan is an online rental listing discovery platform that connects property owners, authorized brokers, and tenants. We are not a party to the tenancy agreements entered into between landlords and tenants.

2. Pakistani Tenancy Laws Compliance

All rental arrangements initiated through this platform must comply with applicable provincial tenancy laws, including the Sindh Rented Premises Ordinance 1979, the Punjab Rented Premises Act 2009, and the Islamabad Rent Restriction Ordinance 2001.

  • A written rent agreement must be registered with the relevant Rent Registrar or Sub-Registrar.
  • Security deposits must be refunded upon expiration or termination of lease subject to clearance of utility bills (K-Electric, LESCO, IESCO, Sui Gas) and inspection for damages.
  • Annual rent escalations must conform to agreed contract terms (typically 10% per annum).

3. Prohibition of Fraud & Fake Listings

Users are strictly prohibited from publishing false pricing, stock photos of non-existent properties, or demanding unverified token advance payments before in-person visits. Violations will result in permanent account termination and referral to law enforcement.
